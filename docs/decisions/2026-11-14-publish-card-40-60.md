# 2026-11-14 — The "Đăng bài" status card at 40/60, compact platform rows, no "Tùy chọn khác" on it

Asked by tuananh219ln from a spec ("Đặc tả UI: Publish Card (tab Đăng bài)"). It changes points of
[the publish panel decision](2026-11-14-publish-panel.md); posting itself does not change.

- **The MP4 takes 2 parts of the card body, its facts 3** (`minmax(0, 2fr) minmax(0, 3fr)`, gap 16), the MP4 filling
  its column at 9:16 (radius 12, ▶ 44 px) instead of a fixed 112 × 199. Below 400 px of card (a container query, as
  before, not the window) it goes above, at most 200 px wide and centred.
- **Platform rows are 34 px with only the badge and the name.** The hint ("Nháp vào hộp thư", "Đã đăng", "Không hẹn
  giờ được", "Đăng tay") is an antd Tooltip and the control's `aria-describedby`. The checkbox stays in the DOM, under
  a ring drawn with `::after`; the picked state comes from React (`data-checked`), not `:has(input:checked)`.
  Posted-by-hand rows (YouTube Shorts, Instagram) are dashed on #fafaf9 with an export mark.
- **Colours stay the theme's** (as the publish panel decision): the spec's #ea580c is `COLORS.primary` (#C2410C),
  #fff7ed is `primaryBg`, the hover #fdba8c is `primaryBorder`.
- **"Tùy chọn khác" leaves the card.** Its drawer (a Page's queue, TikTok posted directly, a post recorded by hand)
  is still needed, so, as the spec says, its button moves to the "Thiết lập" tab: "Tùy chọn đăng khác" there switches
  back to "Đăng bài" and opens the card's drawer, so what was posted shows on the card once it closes. The YouTube
  Shorts and Instagram rows still open that drawer on their tab (the spec's "mở trang ngoài như cũ": "như cũ" is the
  drawer, where the link is recorded; their `aria-label` says "đăng tay, mở ô ghi link bài", not "mở trang ngoài").

No api, data or render change.
