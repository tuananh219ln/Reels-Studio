# 2026-11-09 — A saved template is renamed by its maker only, who is told who uses it before deleting it

Part 5 of the owner's (namtc) review of who may edit, delete and act on what, approved on 2026-11-09. Adds renaming to
"What a member made, only they delete" (2026-11-09, delete by maker), for templates only: a teammate renaming a
template others picked for their new videos confused who used what.

- **Renaming a template** ("Mẫu dựng" on Cấu hình) is its maker's alone (403 for others); the web shows "Đổi tên" and
  "Xoá" only on the maker's own templates. Picking a template for one's own new videos or a group stays open to all.
- **Before deleting**, the maker reads how many members chose it for their new videos and which topic groups hold it
  ("2 người đang chọn làm mẫu cho video mới và 1 nhóm đang giữ (Sức khỏe)"); deleting still clears it everywhere, and
  videos already made keep their look.
- **Text box styles stay as they are** (owner agreed): no maker kept, renamed and deleted by any member.
