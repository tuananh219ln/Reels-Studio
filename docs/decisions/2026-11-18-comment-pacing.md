# 2026-11-18 — Comments by the reel's age: all at once while new, then one a day, none after 3 days

Proposed by the agent and agreed by tuananh219ln, after the first night of detailed comments queued about 130 comments
for one Page in a few hours. It replaces the pacing in [2026-11-18-food-comments](2026-11-18-food-comments.md)
(30–60 s, 45–90 s, 5 minutes between videos) and its 7-day window.

- **A Page's own comments do not lift a reel's reach**; they are worth it for viewers opening the comments, mostly
  in the first hours. So a new reel gets every comment at once, and the rest is spread to keep the Page from bursts.
- **First 24 hours:** the whole run, 45–75 s apart (a 10-dish video in about 12–15 minutes), a minute between two
  videos' runs. Comments can be saved up to 48 hours before a scheduled reel goes live, so the run starts at once.
- **Day 2 to 3:** one new comment a day per reel, at a random time of 9h–21h Vietnam time.
- **After 3 days:** no new comment; a changed text (a product link) is still edited.
- The 100 comments a Page a day stay. No migration: the next daily comment is a request dated ahead, as a rest was.
- The 45–75 s, 9h–21h and 3 days are cautious picks, not limits Facebook publishes; to be tuned from the "Bình luận"
  view after a week.
