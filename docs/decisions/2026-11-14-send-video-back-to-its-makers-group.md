# 2026-11-14 — A group's maker sends a teammate's video back to a group of the video's maker

A group that holds a teammate's video cannot be deleted (the video's maker alone moves or deletes it), so the group's
maker was stuck asking the teammate. The owner chose option (a):

- The maker of the group a video sits in may move it, but only to a group made by the video's maker. Nothing is
  deleted and nobody else's work moves into a group of the mover's choosing.
- Everyone else is still refused; the video's maker keeps moving it anywhere.
- The video payload gains `knowledge_topic_owner_id`; the menus show "Chuyển về nhóm của <tên>".

No migration. Deleting a video and the rest of the ownership rules are unchanged.
