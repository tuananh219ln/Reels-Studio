# 2026-11-28 — The video page's cards, and what an uploaded narration says about itself

Asked by namtc07 from a mock-up of their own; the same header goes on the article's page.

- **Look only, same places.** The header becomes a white bar, the topic line and the plan idea become one context
  card, the progress steps and statuses become chips, and the scenes sit in one card whose head holds their actions
  ("Thêm cảnh" moves there from under the list). "Video này được làm như thế nào" stays, and sums itself up while
  closed. Nothing is taken out of the page.
- **The menu stays the icon rail** of [the fixed header](2026-11-14-video-page-header.md): the mock-up drew a wide
  menu, which would take the width that decision gave to the scenes.
- **An uploaded narration is said where the voice is chosen**, since changing the voice or speed does nothing to it:
  a note on "Âm thanh", a tag on the scene when the sources are mixed, and the voice and speed hidden when every
  scene reads a file. Dropping every file at once is the maker's, as dropping one is.
- **What a new voice or speed costs is said after it is changed** (scenes read again, about the new length), with
  "Hoàn tác". It is worked out on the page from the narration already made; no new request.

No api, data or render change.
