# 2026-11-24 — A title's slots get the scene's ways to take a photo

Each slot of a "Tiêu đề động" now has a small picture (its own photo, or "?" while it follows its scene) and an
"Ảnh ▾" popover with Tải lên, Dán (a copied photo or a photo's link; Ctrl/Cmd+V and drag and drop work too),
Chọn từ kho (Kho tư liệu) and Google with suggested keywords (the slot's name, the name with the title's first
keyword, the matching scene's keywords and its English one). "Xóa ảnh" sends the slot back to its scene's photo.

A slot takes photos only: a TikTok or video link is refused ("Ô tiêu đề chỉ nhận ảnh"). The photo still shows in the
title from the next "Dựng lại tiêu đề", as before: the title is a rendered clip, so a new photo needs a new render.

The Chrome extension sends to the slot a Google search was started from, for ten minutes, then back to the scene
(a search from a scene ends it at once). Slot n matches scene n unless the slot was told to take another one
(`from_scene`).

Not done: the "Đã dùng trong nhóm" list (it is bound to a scene), Pexels, TikTok and AI images for a slot.
