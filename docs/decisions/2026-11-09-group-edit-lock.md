# 2026-11-09 — A topic group's maker may lock its editing; everyone still uses it

Part 4 of the owner's (namtc) review of who may edit, delete and act on what, approved on 2026-11-09. It reverses, for
a group whose maker chooses so, "editing is shared" of "Edits are logged where they are made, and any member puts a
version back" (2026-11-09, edit history) and "Editing a group's templates follows the group's other settings: any
member may" (2026-11-09, group templates).

- **"Cho cả đội sửa"**, a switch per topic group (`knowledge_topics.team_edit`, migration 0071), **on by default**,
  every existing group too: nothing changes until a maker turns it off.
- **Only the group's maker** (`created_by_id`) flips it. Off, only the maker may rename the group, edit any prompt,
  the banned words, the photo hint, the voice, the stock order and the group's templates, change the plan (settings,
  import, replace, "Tạo bằng AI", drop the draft, add, move, edit or delete ideas) and put a version back from
  "Lịch sử". MCP has no tool that edits a group or its plan, so nothing there changes.
- **Using the group stays open to everyone**: making videos from it and from its ideas, reading its prompts, plan and
  history, and the semi-automatic round of a Page that posts it.
- **Only the maker, not a Page owner**: asked on 2026-11-09 whether the owner of a Page that posts a group (and runs
  semi-auto from its plan) also edits it while locked, the owner answered "Không, chỉ chủ nhóm".
- **"Nhận nhóm"**: a group with no known maker (as "Sức khỏe" may be, 2026-11-09 delete by maker) is claimed by the
  first member who presses it, as "Nhận Page" does for a Page; a group with a maker keeps it.
