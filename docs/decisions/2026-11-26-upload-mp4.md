# 2026-11-26 — "MP4 tải lên": a finished MP4 is a video of its own kind, posted as it is

Asked by the owner, from the spec "Tải lên MP4 hoàn chỉnh + tool MCP cho Muse" (26/11/2026, Looper). Group 13 "Everyday
Health Guide – US" has Muse build the whole chalkboard video outside the Studio; the Studio could only turn a script into
scenes, so Muse's MP4 had nowhere to go (video #433 became an empty "Video xuyên suốt").

- **A third mode, `videos.mode = "upload"`.** No scenes, never rendered. The file is `mp4_key`, so every posting route,
  the Page queue and "Tải MP4" read it like any other MP4. `render_signature` of an upload is the file's own SHA-256, so
  `mp4_outdated` is never true and the review gate's approval holds for exactly this file. Columns (migration 0130):
  `source_mp4` (`storage_key`, `url`, `size_bytes`, `sha256`, `uploaded_at`, `uploaded_by`), `media_info` (ffprobe),
  `upload_note`, `upload_key` (idempotency). The title is `name`; the pinned comment is the usual shape (title, then the
  "Dòng khảo sát" = the `pinned_comment_text` sent), because an upload has no scenes to list.
- **One module, [upload_mp4.py](../../api/src/reels_api/upload_mp4.py),** for the web (`POST /api/videos/upload`,
  `/upload-from-url`, `PUT /api/videos/{id}/source-mp4`) and Muse (MCP `upload_finished_video`,
  `replace_finished_video`, `schedule_video_at`). Hard checks (`400 invalid_media` with the reason): not an MP4, codec not
  H.264/HEVC, short side under 540 px, length under 3 s or over 90 s; `413 file_too_large` over 200 MB. Soft findings
  (HEVC, not 9:16 or not 1080×1920, outside 15–60 s, no audio) are stored as the MP4's QC `warn` checks, so they reach the
  review gate's `warn` items (acknowledge to approve) and the sender's `warnings`; banned words in the title, caption and
  pinned comment warn the same way. The spec's acceptance line "16:9 is refused" contradicts its own table (aspect ratio is
  soft); the table was followed.
- **The review gate stays in front of scheduling (does not reverse [2026-11-26-review-gate](2026-11-26-review-gate.md)).**
  The spec's `schedule: "suggested"` straight from the upload and "Thay MP4 keeps the schedule" cannot hold with a gate
  that approves one MP4 for one fingerprint. So an upload starts `pending_review`; `schedule` other than `none` only adds a
  warning telling Muse to ask the member and call `approve_video` (`publish: "schedule"`); `schedule_video_at` posts an
  approved video at a time (default: the idea's suggested time, `fanpage_id` required) and is refused `not_approved`
  otherwise; replacing the MP4 sends the video back to review and withdraws what was planned (the same `after_render`).
  If the owner wants Muse's uploads to skip review, that is a new decision.
- **Plan sync is free:** `item_status` already reads the linked video and its posts, so an idea shows Đã tạo video, Chờ
  đăng, Đã hẹn giờ and Đã đăng from the video, and deleting the video unlinks the idea. An idea that already has a video is
  `409 plan_item_taken` unless `replace_existing` (the old video stays, unlinked). `idempotency_key` returns the video made
  with it in the last 24 hours.
- **The link is downloaded inside the request, not by a job** (the spec asked for `202` + a job). It is capped at 200 MB
  and about 110 s, like "Thêm vào kho từ link" already downloads inline; the MCP tool waits for it anyway. A job and a
  `202` can replace it if downloads of that size time out in production.
- **MCP's `schedule_video` already exists** (adds to a Page's queue, no time), so the spec's time-picking tool is
  `schedule_video_at`.
- **Not built:** "Nhân bản" of an upload (refused `upload_video`; the spec wanted a copy sharing the file), `apply_logo`
  (the MP4 is posted without the Page's logo, as the spec's default), a TikTok version of an upload, turning video #433
  (or any scene video) into an upload, and keeping the original file until 30 days after posting (it is kept until the
  video is deleted).
