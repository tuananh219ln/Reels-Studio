# Scene clips accept a 576 px short side

Date: 2026-11-11

An uploaded scene clip now needs at least 576 px on its short side (was 720), so a 576×1024 clip,
a common 9:16 export, is accepted instead of refused with "Video phải có cạnh ngắn từ 720 px".
The long side stays at most 4096 px. The render still scales every clip to the 1080×1920 frame, so a
576 px clip looks softer than a 720 px one; the team chose to accept that rather than re-export.
`cut_part` raises a smaller footage cut (a 360p YouTube video) to the same 576 px minimum.
