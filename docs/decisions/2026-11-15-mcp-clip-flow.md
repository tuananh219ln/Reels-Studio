# 2026-11-15 — The MCP clip flow: whole-stretch checks, text and stickers over a blur, a group's "no people", music by link

Asked and approved by the owner (namtc) on 2026-11-15, after fixing videos 158, 160 and 162 by hand through MCP (find
TikTok links in a browser, download to Kho tư liệu, look, attach, cut, blur, render, check) and after video 160 came
out with strangers eating in its clips. The owner chose that the assistants (T3, Friday/Muse) run this flow by message,
not the server's automatic footage job, whose decision of 2026-11-13 stands unchanged (TikTok search is still not
shipped there; the switch stays off).

- **Two new MCP tools and a field**: "Xem nhiều khung hình của clip" (a grid over the stretch to be used) and "Gắn clip
  cho cảnh" (attach, cut, blur, keep the scene's length, answer with its first frame); "Sửa cảnh" moves a scene's own
  text. Details in [chatgpt.md](../chatgpt.md).
- **Edit tools answer compactly**, since the full video they returned was most of an assistant's token use; reading a
  video and making one still answer in full.
- **A blur is covered**: the scene's text moves onto the blurred patch and cover stickers take what it leaves showing,
  added by the tool itself (owner: "tự thêm"), never on a scene without text. Only the kitchen set and Trái tim, Ngôi
  sao, Dấu tích cover: word badges and Dấu X do not suit a health page.
- **Six kitchen stickers** in the render core (Lá xanh, Bát canh, Đôi đũa, Chén trà, Quả táo, Bông hoa), drawn like the
  others, offered everywhere stickers are. A render-core change: done when a person has watched
  [samples/sticker-nha-bep.json](../../samples/sticker-nha-bep.json) rendered.
- **"Không dùng cảnh có người" is a group's switch**, on for "Ăn Lành Sống Khỏe" only: the Threads pages' Mukbang vibe
  needs people, and Già Mà Khỏe was left as it is. The assistants follow it now; finding people automatically, with a
  warning that holds back auto-approval and scheduling (not rendering), is a later change.
- **Music by link** into the team's library, licensed MP3s only, with the licence noted; never TikTok or YouTube.
- **The video page**: "Tùy chọn đăng khác" moves back from "Thiết lập" to "Đăng bài", under the status card, and the side
  panel loses its own scroll, growing with the page with its tabs held under the header. This replaces, in
  [2026-11-14-video-editor-spec.md](2026-11-14-video-editor-spec.md) and the decisions on the side panel of that day,
  the button's place in "Thiết lập" and the panel's scroll.
