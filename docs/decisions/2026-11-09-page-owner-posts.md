# 2026-11-09 — Only a Page's owner posts to it, and may take any post on it down

Approved by the owner (namtc) on 2026-11-09 (option A), in the review of who may edit, delete and act on what: the
tool is shared but the money is per member, and a post's commission belongs to its Page's owner (2026-10-31), so a
teammate posting on a Page they do not own put their work, or their mistakes, on someone else's income.

- **Posting is the owner's**: a video ("Đăng bài" › Facebook), an article, and "Thêm vào hàng chờ" to a chosen Page are
  refused to any other member (403, "Chỉ chủ Page “…” (<chủ>) mới đăng được lên Page này"). The Page queue's own
  changes and the semi-automatic approval were already the owner's. A Page with no owner (`owner_id` null) is refused
  to everyone, pointing to "Nhận Page" on Quản lý Page.
- **The Page pickers offer only the member's own Pages**. This replaces, for posting, "the Page pickers for posting
  and the queue list my Pages first … “Trang khác” keep every Page in reach" of "A member's own Pages and topic
  groups come first where one is picked" (2026-11-08): "Trang khác" is gone from those pickers. Quản lý Page's
  "Tất cả" still lists every Page, so a teammate's Page and its owner stay in sight.
- **Taking a post down**: still whoever posted it, and now also the Page's owner, for a post a teammate made on their
  Page before this rule (narrows nothing of "What a member made, only they delete", 2026-11-09: it adds the owner).
- Unchanged: TikTok goes through the member's own connection, and YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels are recorded by
  hand with no Page. MCP has no posting tool.
