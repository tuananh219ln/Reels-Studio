# Lists of videos: 5 a page, the member picks more

Every list of videos or posts (`VideoTable`: Video, Bài đăng, a group's Video tab, a Page's Nội dung, Affiliate's
videos) shows 5 rows a page until the member picks 10, 15, 20, 50 or 100 under the table. The choice is kept in the
browser (localStorage, `usePageSize`), shared by all those lists, and survives a reload. The owner asked for a default
of 5 (it was a fixed 50). A list that fits in 5 rows shows no pager.
