# 2026-11-20 — The total-length block moves from 90 to 150 seconds

Reverses "The 90-second block stays" (2026-11-01, "Scripts may run to 150 words, about 52 seconds").

- **Block:** the pre-render check and the render job block a video whose total (title scene included, scenes
  lengthened to fit their narration) is over 150 seconds, instead of 90 (`MAX_TOTAL_SECONDS`).
- **Footage:** a video cut from footage shares the constant, so it keeps the first 150 seconds of stretches instead
  of the first 90.
- **Unchanged:** the yellow warning stays at 20–55 seconds, so a script between 55 and 150 seconds renders with
  only a warning; a scene is still 1–10 seconds. A longer video costs more render time and TTS, and may lose
  viewers before the end.
